**Dr. Elizabeth Pinel** is a Full Professor of Psychological Science at the University of Vermont. She earned her Ph.D. from the University of Texas at Austin, and held a faculty position at Penn State University before joining UVM's faculty. Dr. Pinel's research focuses on the self, with earlier lines of research on stigma consciousness and predicting future emotional experiences, and more recent work focusing on experiences of connection vs. isolation.
